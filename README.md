## 👋 Who am I?

**I solve real business problems by designing and building scalable digital solutions.**

My professional journey spans business and financial management, IT infrastructure and security, data and analytics, and software development. This cross-disciplinary experience has shaped how I approach technology — starting with the business problem, understanding the system behind it, and then designing the right digital solution.

Currently, I focus on building software products and digital solutions, combining business understanding, systems thinking, and hands-on development to turn ideas and real-world problems into scalable products and systems.

- 🧩 **Software Product Development** — Building practical digital products around real business needs.
- 🔐 **Authentication & Digital Identity** — Building secure, flexible, and scalable approaches to identity and authentication.
- 🏗️ **Software Architecture & Systems Thinking** — Designing maintainable systems with clear boundaries and long-term evolution in mind.
- 📊 **Data, Analytics & AI** — Developing my capabilities in data-driven problem solving and exploring how AI can enhance products, decisions, and workflows.
- 💼 **Business & Technology** — Understanding where technology can create meaningful value for real business problems.

## 📦 Featured Projects

### Ceebl

I'm building [**Ceebl**](https://ceebl.ir/), a product ecosystem focused on digital identity, authentication, and digital trust.

The first product, **Ceebl Authentication**, is a modular authentication platform designed to provide secure and flexible authentication experiences across digital platforms. Its first integration targets the Joomla ecosystem, with broader platform support planned as the product evolves.

Ceebl is where I put many of my ideas about software architecture, product development, security, and business-driven technology into practice.

### 🌟 Open-Source Collaboration

#### Beyond Coding — Persian Translation

I'm working on the Persian translation of **Beyond Coding** by David Tuffley, an open educational project aimed at making professional development knowledge more accessible to Persian-speaking technology professionals.

The project follows a structured translation and editorial workflow, with documented translation guidelines, terminology management, licensing, and an open contribution process.

This project is also part of my broader commitment to learning in public, contributing to open knowledge, and becoming more actively involved in open-source communities.

➡️ [**Explore the project**](https://github.com/hsaboury/beyond-coding-persian)

## 🤝 Community & Ecosystem

### Motamem

For more than 4,000 days, I have been a member of [**Motamem**](https://motamem.org/), a Persian learning community founded and led by [**Mohammadreza Shabanali**](https://mrshabanali.com/), focused on continuous learning, professional development, management, and deeper thinking about business and life.

Over the years, Motamem has become an important part of my learning journey — not only as a source of knowledge, but as a community that has influenced how I think, learn, and approach professional growth.

My long-term participation in this community reflects something I deeply value: learning as a continuous process rather than a destination.

### Joomla Ecosystem

I have a long-standing connection with the Joomla ecosystem and am now becoming more actively involved in its community and open-source development.

I'm building [**Joomlog**](https://joomlog.ir/), a Persian platform focused on strengthening the Joomla ecosystem in Iran through knowledge sharing, community interaction, and practical resources.

As part of this journey, I plan to contribute more directly to the broader Joomla community through testing, feedback, open-source contributions, and collaboration with other community members.
